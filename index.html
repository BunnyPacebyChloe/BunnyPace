Google Drive sync

Saves your projects in a file called BunnyPace-data.json in your own Google Drive, so Mac and iPhone stay in sync. The app can only see files it creates itself.

Backup

Data is saved on this device. Export a backup to keep a copy or move it between Mac and iPhone, then restore it on the other device (it merges, nothing is overwritten).

1. Load your estimate file

Upload your estimate as Excel, CSV, Word (.docx), PDF, or a screenshot/photo. Word, PDF and images are read right in your browser (images use text recognition, so check the preview carefully). Bunny Pace picks out the milestones (with their hour ranges) and only the lettered tasks (a., b., c.), then shows you the result to confirm before loading. Recorded sessions of tasks with the same name are kept. This only affects the project you have open.
